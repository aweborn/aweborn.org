Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Aweborn is a non-profit research organization. We collect what the game needs to work and, as described below, what helps us study how people explore and create together. We never sell your information, and there are no ads and no third-party trackers.

This policy covers aweborn.org and its services (together, “Aweborn”). “We” and “us” mean Aweborn.

What we collect

  • Your player. When you first visit, we create an anonymous player for you, identified by a random ID. No name or email is needed to play.
  • Sessions. We store a sign-in cookie for sync.aweborn.org so you stay the same player when you come back. With each session we record the IP address and browser type, to keep accounts secure and to spot abuse.
  • If you save your player with Google, Google shares your name, email address and profile picture with us. We use them only to sign you in and to show you which methods are linked. We never post anything to your Google account.
  • If you save your player with a passkey, we store the passkey’s public key and a device label. Your fingerprint, face or PIN never leaves your device; we never see it.
  • If you save your player with your email, we store your email address and use it only to send you sign-in codes and important notices about your player. We never send marketing. If you also choose a password, we store only a secure hash of it, never the password itself.
  • What you create. Worlds, objects and other content you build are stored on our servers and are visible to other players.
  • Making things with AI. When you use the world planner or create an object, we keep everything about it: what you typed, every reply, every version of your plan, which AI model answered, what each request cost, and the models, sounds and other files that were made. We keep it so we can rebuild how a creation came together, step by step, for our research and to make creating better (see “Research” below). It is linked to your random player ID, not to your name or email. Don’t type personal information into the planner.
  • Your World Index. We record the worlds your player has found: whether you’ve seen or visited each one, how you got there (by flying, a link, a card or a warp), how many times you’ve visited, and when you first and last visited. This is your collection; it follows you across devices.
  • Live presence. While you play, your position and player colour are shared in real time with other players nearby. Today we don’t keep a history of where you moved; if that changes, see “Research” below.
  • World chat. What you say in a world’s chat is shown to everyone in that world (the most recent 200 lines are kept with the world, so people who arrive later can read them), tagged with your random player ID. We filter language for display, masking some words for readers. We also keep a permanent copy of every message as you wrote it (unmasked), with the world and your random player ID, for our research (see “Research” below). It is plain text. Don’t put personal information in chat.
  • Space chat. Out in space (at Origin and anywhere outside a world), what you say is shown to everyone flying in the universe, tagged with your random player ID. The server holds the most recent 200 lines in memory so people who arrive later can read them (they’re gone when the server restarts). Same filtering as world chat, and the same permanent research copy, marked “space” instead of a world.
  • On your device. We keep a few settings in your browser’s local storage, for example whether we’ve already reminded you to save your player and how long you’ve played. They stay on your device.
  • Donations. Payments are handled by Stripe. Your card details go straight to Stripe and never touch our servers. We receive a record of the donation (amount, and the email you gave Stripe). Every donation also becomes mana in Aweborn’s public pool: its amount and date appear in the public ledger, but never your name or email.
  • Server logs. Like any website, our servers log requests (including IP address) for security and debugging.

How we use it

  • To run the game: keep your player, sync shared worlds, and let you sign in on other devices.
  • To keep Aweborn safe: prevent abuse, investigate problems, and enforce our Terms.
  • To process and acknowledge donations.
  • To improve the game and for our research, using information combined across many players (see below).

We don’t sell or rent your information, we don’t run ads, we never use it for advertising, and we don’t use third-party analytics or trackers.

Research

Aweborn exists to study how people build trust and work well together, so that teams everywhere can be formed more humanely. You can read about it in our research plan. The game is how we learn: how players explore, create and collaborate.

  • What we study: gameplay only, such as which worlds people find, what they create, who they create with and how journeys through the game unfold. Never anything from outside Aweborn.
  • How: we analyse patterns across many players. Research data is keyed to random IDs and kept separate from your sign-in details (name, email). We study journeys, not individuals.
  • How creations come together: we study the full history of making things with AI (your messages, the planner’s replies, each version of the plan and what was made) and can replay it step by step. This history is keyed to your random player ID.
  • How people talk: we study world and space chat, including the original, unmasked text of every message, to understand how people communicate and collaborate. Each message is keyed to the world (or “space”) and your random player ID.
  • Before we record more: today our research uses only the information listed above. Before we start recording more detailed gameplay (for example movement histories), we’ll update this policy with exactly what we record and tell you in the game.
  • Always your choice: we will never build a profile of you as an identified person, such as a personality read-out or matching you into a team outside the game, without asking you first. You can say no, and change your mind later.
  • Publishing: we may share or publish findings and data sets that are combined across players and can’t reasonably identify anyone. When we work with a university, its research ethics board reviews the study.

Who else handles it

  • Amazon Web Services: hosting, content delivery, encrypted backups, and sending our emails through Amazon SES (US).
  • Google: only if you choose “Continue with Google”. Our pages also load fonts from Google Fonts, which sees your IP address.
  • Stripe: only if you donate.
  • AI providers: when you make something with AI, what you typed (and, for the planner, the conversation so far and your current plan) is sent to process that request. Planner messages go through OpenRouter to the model that answers; depending on the model, that is Anthropic, Google, OpenAI, xAI or Moonshot AI. 3D models are made by Meshy. We send no name, email or sign-in details, only the request itself, and we choose settings that don’t allow providers to train on it where they’re offered.
  • Have I Been Pwned: if you choose a password, we check whether it has appeared in a known data breach. Only the first 5 characters of a hash of it are sent, so the service never learns your password.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect players and the public from harm.

How long we keep it

  • Sessions expire after a year without use, or right away when you sign out.
  • Your player, your World Index and saved sign-in methods are kept until you ask us to delete them.
  • In a world, chat shows only the latest 200 lines; older lines are dropped from the world as new ones arrive. Our permanent research copy of chat is kept until you ask us to delete it with your player.
  • The history of what you made with AI is kept for research until you ask us to delete it with your player. Data sets we’ve already published can’t be recalled, but they never identify you.
  • When you remove something you made from the library, it disappears for everyone but moves to a private trash that only Aweborn’s administrators can see. It stays there until we delete it forever, or until you ask us to delete it with your player.
  • Backups are kept for up to 90 days, then permanently deleted.
  • Donation records are kept as long as the law requires for non-profit accounting.

Your choices and rights

  • You can play without ever giving us your name or email.
  • You can sign out of any device from the “Save your player” panel.
  • You can ask us for a copy of your information, to correct it, or to delete your player and everything linked to it. Email us (below) and we’ll respond within 30 days. Deleted data leaves our backups within 90 days.
  • Depending on where you live (for example the EU, the UK or California), you may have more rights under local law. We honour those requests too.

Security

All traffic is encrypted (HTTPS). Sign-in codes expire after 10 minutes and are stored only as hashes. Passwords are stored only as secure hashes, and we don’t store any password for Google or passkey sign-in. Backups are encrypted and access is restricted. No system is perfect, but we work to protect what you trust us with.

Age

Aweborn contains content made by other players and is meant for people 18 and older. We don’t knowingly collect information from children. If you think a child has given us information, contact us and we’ll delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we’ll update the date above. If a change is significant, we’ll let you know in the game.

Contact

Questions or requests about your privacy: privacy@aweborn.org. Anything else: hello@aweborn.org. A real person reads every message.
