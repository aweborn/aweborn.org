Where your gift goes

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Aweborn is a 501(c)(3) non-profit. Every dollar that comes in becomes mana in the universe’s pool, so the pool is a live picture of all the money Aweborn has. Every gift, from anyone, is shared out the same way, and half of it is the creative energy that lets every player bring their ideas to life.

The split

50% 20% 30%
  • 50% Creative share, for everyone The compute behind every creation in the game: meshes, terrain, sounds, whole worlds.
  • 20% Research Our mission: learning how people build trust and work well together. Read the plan.
  • 30% People & operations Running the servers, keeping players safe, support, and finding more supporters.

This is our planned split. Our board reviews it every year, and we’ll update this page whenever it changes.

How mana works

  • One mana is one cent. Every dollar in (gifts, card sales, grants) adds 100 mana to the pool. Every dollar out draws from it, and every draw is labeled: creations, servers, people, research.
  • Players create with the creative share. When a player manifests something, the universe draws exactly what the compute costs us, with no markup, and only from the creative half. Salaries and servers can never be spent on creations, and creations can never eat into salaries.
  • Mana is shared. Nobody owns mana, not even donors. The bigger the creative share, the faster everyone can create.
  • An example: a $10 gift is about $9.41 after card fees. That adds about 941 mana to the pool, and about 470 of it is creative mana for the whole universe.
  • The universe grows. Every dollar in also pushes the Living Frontier outward. A golden wave sweeps from the center of the cosmos, and dreamed-up worlds become solid.

The in-game mana pool is still being built. Until it’s live, the creative half pays for the servers and compute that already run the universe.

What a gift doesn’t buy

No mana of your own, no abilities, no levels, no advantage over anyone. Abilities are earned by playing. The one thing we offer donors is a thank-you you can opt into: your name on the golden wave (“A wave of light from ★ you”), or simply “a generous soul”.

Why half

  • Most of every gift goes to the mission. Mana and research together are 70%. The BBB Wise Giving Alliance asks charities to spend at least 65% on their programs and no more than 35% of contributions on fundraising (BBB Standards for Charity Accountability).
  • But not by starving the organization. When non-profits squeeze overhead to look lean, they underinvest in the people and systems they need to do good work (Gregory & Howard, “The Nonprofit Starvation Cycle”, Stanford Social Innovation Review, 2009).
  • One honest number. Donors care where their money goes, and giving rises when people know their gift isn’t paying for overhead (Gneezy, Keenan & Gneezy, “Avoiding overhead aversion in charity”, Science, 2014). So we keep the split simple and public, and if a founding grant ever covers our operations, we’ll raise the mana share of everyone else’s gifts.
  • Impact, not just ratios. Overhead ratios are easy to judge and impact is hard, so people tend to over-weight the ratio (Caviola et al., “The evaluability bias in charitable giving”, Judgment and Decision Making, 2014). That’s why you can see your gift in the game.
