Who can play

You must be 18 or older. Worlds are made by players, and some of them may contain mature content.

Your player

  • You can play anonymously. If you save your player (with a passkey, Google or your email), you’re responsible for keeping access to that account and device secure.
  • An anonymous player lives in your browser. If you clear its data before saving, we may not be able to recover it.
  • One person, one player is the spirit of the game. Don’t impersonate others or use automated tools to flood the cosmos.

What you create

  • You keep ownership of the worlds and content you make.
  • So that Aweborn can work, you give us a worldwide, royalty-free licence to store, display, copy and share your content within Aweborn, and to show it when we promote Aweborn (for example in screenshots or on trading cards). This licence ends when the content is deleted, except for copies already shared or kept in backups for up to 90 days.
  • Who can change a world: a world’s creator, and anyone they make a builder, can move or remove anything in it and shape its terrain and sky. Everyone else can add things, kick things around, and move or remove only what they placed themselves. The game puts back changes that break these rules.
  • You can remove what you made from the shared library. It then disappears for everyone, but is held in a private trash that only our administrators can see until we delete it forever. Copies already placed in worlds keep working until we delete the original.
  • Content you place in shared spaces is visible to other players, who may build around it.
  • Only upload or create things you have the right to share.

Making things with AI

  • The world planner and creation tools (objects, pictures, sounds, music, voice lines and lore) use AI services. What they make can be surprising, imperfect or similar to other things; look before you share it.
  • The community rules below apply to what you ask for, not just what you place. Don’t ask for anything sexual, hateful or gory, real people, or other people’s characters, brands or art.
  • Pictures, sounds and lore signs you place can be seen and heard by everyone who visits that world, and a world’s creators can give it its own sound. A voice line or a sign follows the same rules as chat.
  • Each request uses mana from the shared pool. A request that fails uses nothing.
  • The AI tools are for players who have signed in (saved their player). Anyone can explore, enter worlds and build with blocks and the shared library without signing in.
  • Everyone who is signed in can make a limited amount each day, and when many people are making at once the pool is shared fairly between them. We may change these limits as we learn from play, and when we do we’ll update this page.
  • Your messages, the replies and what gets made are kept for research, as described in our Privacy Policy. We may review them to keep Aweborn safe.

Community rules

Don’t use Aweborn to:

  • harass, threaten or bully anyone, or promote hatred against people for who they are;
  • share sexual content involving minors, or any content that sexualises minors, in any form;
  • share illegal content, or other people’s private information;
  • infringe anyone’s copyright, trademark or other rights;
  • spread malware, phish, scam or spam;
  • break, overload or get around the security of Aweborn or other players’ accounts.

Mature content made by adults for adults may be allowed in worlds, but we may restrict where it appears.

These rules apply to everything you say in world chat, too. Chat is plain text; we filter some language, limit how fast you can send, and may remove messages or limit chat for anyone who breaks the rules.

Moderation

We may remove content, or limit, suspend or end access, if we believe it breaks these terms, puts others at risk or exposes Aweborn to legal harm. Where we can, we’ll explain why. If you think we got it wrong, contact us.

Reporting

In the game, tap a player’s name in chat to mute them (their chat disappears for you) or report them; a person on our team reviews every report, and the reported player is never told who reported them. To report content, or a copyright problem, you can also email us (below) with a description and where in the cosmos it is. We act on valid copyright notices.

Donations

Aweborn is a 501(c)(3) non-profit. Donations support our mission and are processed by Stripe. Every donation (after payment fees) becomes mana in the universe’s pool, which reflects all the money Aweborn has. Half of it is the creative share all players create with; the rest funds our research and operations (where your gift goes). If operating costs ever exceed the rest of the pool, the creative share covers the difference, and we show each time this happens in the game. A donation doesn’t buy you mana, abilities or any advantage of your own. Donations may be tax-deductible to the extent the law allows; check with your tax adviser. Donations are voluntary and generally non-refundable. If something went wrong with a payment, contact us and we’ll make it right.

Research

Aweborn is also a research project about how people build trust and work well together. Gameplay helps that research, as explained in our Privacy Policy, which describes what we record, how it’s protected and where you get a choice.

Trading cards

Aweborn trading cards are collectibles that link to places in the cosmos: tap one and you enter its world. They’re not investments and have no guaranteed monetary value.

Ordering cards. You can order printed cards of a world you’ve visited that is still in the universe, at the price shown before you pay (now $9 a card, shipping included). For now we ship only within the United States. Stripe handles the payment. A person on our team checks every order before it’s printed, and we may cancel an order, with a full refund, if the world’s name or art, or a photo you add, breaks our community rules or the world is no longer in the universe. Groups, schools and events can arrange larger orders with us directly (email us; see the event guide): we agree the price before anything is printed, and the same checks apply. You can cancel for a full refund any time before your cards are printed: email us with the link to your order. After printing, we replace or refund cards that arrive damaged or don’t work. If you add your own photo from photo mode, it must be your own shot of the game, and you let us print it on your cards. A card shows the world as it was when it was printed. Worlds change, and if a world is removed, its card stops leading there. Buying a card is a purchase, not a donation; like a donation, the money becomes mana in the universe’s pool (where it goes).
