Card orders

Paid orders wait for a person: check the world’s name and art are fine to print forever, then approve. Printing and shipping take the real cost (it’s drawn from the pool as a gold star). Shipping addresses are in Stripe: open the payment.

    Card sets

    A set groups worlds in everyone’s World Index (“7 / 12 discovered”) and prints its name and number on the cards. Print a whole set with node scripts/cards/build-batch.mjs --set <id> --qty 50. Sets are public: only add worlds you’re happy for everyone to see listed.

      Event orders

      Cards for an event, a school, a shop: no checkout. The order is created already approved (you’re the person check) and prints with build-batch.mjs --orders. Origin is allowed here. If the organiser paid you (invoice, cash), enter what you received after fees: it goes into the pool once, like a card sale.
