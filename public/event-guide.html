Aweborn cards at your event

A one-page guide for hosts. Keep it on your phone or print it.

Each card has a tiny chip inside. Tap it to a phone and that person lands inside the card’s world: no app, no account, no sign-up.

Everyone who taps the same card arrives in the same place, so a room full of people ends up exploring, building and talking together within a minute or two.

1Before the day

  1. Get the cards. Email hello@aweborn.org with how many, which world (Origin, or one you’ve built) and your date. We check, print and mail them (US for now).
  2. Test one card on an iPhone and an Android phone, in the room if you can. If both land in the world, you’re set.
  3. Check the signal. The game loads over cellular. Room Wi-Fi helps when a big group taps in at once.
  4. Optional: a set. Several worlds can become a set, and everyone’s World Index then shows how many they’ve found (“3 / 12 discovered”). Ask us when you order.

2Handing them out

What to say

“Unlock your phone and hold the card flat against the back: near the top on an iPhone, the middle on most Android phones. Tap the notification if one pops up.”

“Nothing? Open your camera and scan the little QR code in the corner.”

3Once they’re in

  • Move: on a phone, the left stick walks and the right stick looks around. On a computer, the keyboard button lists the keys.
  • Talk: Chat reaches everyone in the world. Anyone can mute or report someone.
  • Make: Make → Library to pick up blocks and shapes and place them.
  • Collect: every world you find becomes a card in your World Index.
  • Keep it: More → Save your player, so it follows you to another device. Playing never needs it.

4If a tap doesn’t work

Nothing happens on an iPhone
The screen has to be on and unlocked. iPhone XS or newer. Take off a thick or metal case.
Nothing happens on Android
Turn on NFC: open Settings and search “NFC”.
It opens a browser tab
That’s right: the game runs in the browser.
Slow to load
Weak signal. Join the Wi-Fi or move toward a window; the loading bar shows how far along it is.
Still stuck
Scan the QR code, or go to aweborn.org and tap World Index.

5After the event

The cards keep working. The world stays in the universe, and a tap months later still drops you in. For more cards, email hello@aweborn.org.
