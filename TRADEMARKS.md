# Aweborn Trademark Policy

Aweborn's code is open source ([MIT](LICENSE)) and its docs are open ([CC BY 4.0](docs/LICENSE)). Take them, change them and build your own thing. **The name and logo are the one part that isn't open.** They're how people know something really comes from Aweborn, the non-profit. That trust belongs to the people who play, give and build here, and this policy protects it.

## What this covers

- The name **Aweborn™** (and *aweborn.org*)
- The Aweborn logo ([`aweborn-logo.svg`](aweborn-logo.svg), [`aweborn-logo.png`](aweborn-logo.png), the favicon and app icons in [`public/`](public/))
- Anything confusingly similar to them

## You can, without asking

- **Talk about Aweborn:** write about it, review it, teach it, criticize it, link to it.
- **Say where your code came from:** "based on Aweborn", "a fork of Aweborn", "uses code from Aweborn".
- **Run an unmodified copy** for yourself or to test, as long as it isn't presented as the official Aweborn.
- **Use the logo to link to aweborn.org** or to show that you support the project, without changing it.

## Please ask first

- Events, meetups or community groups with "Aweborn" in the name
- Merchandise, cards or anything sold with the name or logo

Write to hello@aweborn.org or [open an issue](https://github.com/aweborn/aweborn.org/issues). We like saying yes.

## You can't

- **Name a fork, product, game, company or domain "Aweborn"** or anything confusingly similar. Forks are welcome and must pick their own name and logo.
- **Imply that Aweborn endorses, sponsors or runs** something it doesn't.
- **Raise money in Aweborn's name.** Every gift to Aweborn goes through aweborn.org.
- **Change the logo** and still call it Aweborn's.

## Why only the name

Ideas can't be owned, and we don't want to own them: the code, the business plan and the design are public so anyone can build on them. Aweborn exists because we're born with a sense of awe, and chasing it is how we find freedom. The name is how people find the real thing.

Questions or reports of misuse: hello@aweborn.org.
