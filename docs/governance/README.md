organization is run and hold it to its own rules.

[Bylaws](./bylaws.md) and the
[Conflict of Interest Policy](./conflict-of-interest-policy.md) by unanimous
written consent on November 19, 2026, and the new, independent-majority

## What gets published, and what doesn't

**Published:** documents that set the rules or that the law already makes
public (articles, bylaws, policies, the IRS determination letter, the
exemption application, annual returns), plus who serves on the board.

**Kept private,** in Aweborn's records, not here:

- Signed originals: written consents, director acceptances, resignations
- Directors' conflict-of-interest disclosures and personal financial details
- Minutes or consents that discuss an individual's pay or personal matters
  (pay is reported publicly on the annual return, in the IRS format)
- Bank, payment-processor and account details, credentials, and keys

## Redaction before posting

Remove personal phone numbers, personal email addresses and home
addresses of directors and incorporators. Keep the EIN (41-4945118), the
organization's own address and the registered agent: those are public
anyway, and donors need the EIN.

## Keeping it current

- Post the adopted text, never a draft presented as in force.
- Any change to the bylaws or policies is adopted by the board first, then
  posted in the same commit as a note in the history below.
- `board.md` changes whenever a director or officer joins or leaves.
